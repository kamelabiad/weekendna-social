## 3. Positioning and audience
Promise: **Discover your next Lebanese weekend escape.** Be a helpful discovery guide, not merely a property catalogue.
Primary: Lebanese couples, friends and families planning stays; secondary: diaspora and visiting travellers. Owner-facing posts are a separate business objective and should not dominate the consumer feed.

### Content mix (guideline, not a forced quota)
- **40% discovery:** real hidden gems, winter escapes, location-led reveals.
- **30% useful comparisons:** budget, group size, proximity, cosy options.
- **20% standout property showcases:** authentic atmosphere and distinctive value.
- **10% community/trust:** guest FAQs, genuine experiences, thoughtful polls.

## 4. Production standard
- Hook within **0–2 seconds** using the strongest real visual, with concise specific English text; no logo intro.
- Reel test length **8–15 seconds**, adjusted by actual retention; do not force weak still-photo slideshows into Reels.
- Show destination/property identity, then a clear benefit, then **one** CTA: save, share or follow.
- Use verified nightly price, capacity, location and amenities only. Do not claim snow, availability or travel times without verification.
- Use properly licensed audio on Reels; do not change music against existing playbook rules.
- Carousels: **1080×1350**; Reels/Stories: **1080×1920**; follow BRAND.md exactly (official logo, Plus Jakarta Sans, approved palette).
- Avoid repetitive caption formulae such as “Your weekend, found for you” and long sales-heavy feature dumps. English only.
- Each post needs a distinctive concept and reason to save/share; do not republish near-identical assets or saturate a property.

## 6. Distribution and collaborations

## 7. Decision rules
- **Skip >70%:** opening likely underperforming; test first shot, hook or pacing; do not repeat unchanged.
- **Skip 50–70%:** inspect watch time, saves/shares and reach before deciding.
- **Skip <50%:** promising first-frame signal; still check follows and useful engagement.
- **Low reach + good engagement:** test distribution, partner collaborations, timing.
- **High reach + weak follows:** review audience relevance, profile promise and follow CTA.
- **High views + few saves/shares:** improve usefulness and specificity.
These are testing heuristics, not proven algorithm rules. Change one or two variables per test.
