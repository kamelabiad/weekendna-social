Found a place you love? Here is how to request it, in 4 steps 🏡
1. Pick your dates on the listing's calendar.
2. Send a request: WhatsApp opens with your dates, number of guests and request number already written.
3. The owner replies to you directly and confirms.
4. You never pay Weekendna: no online payment and no booking fee. You agree the deposit with the owner, usually by bank transfer, OMT/Whish or cash on arrival.
No reply within 48 hours? The request expires, so you can book another place.
