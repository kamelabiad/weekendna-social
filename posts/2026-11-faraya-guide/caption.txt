Planning a weekend in Faraya? Here is a short guide 🔥⛰️
Found on Weekendna: two Faraya stays, then what to see nearby.
1. Acampar Faraya, 45 min from Beirut: wooden bungalows, most with a fireplace, mountain views and a terrace, for 2 to 6 guests. From $80/night.
2. Faraya Village Club, Mzaar: a chimney in every chalet and a heated pool on site. From $150/night.
Nearby: Faqra Natural Bridge (a 38 m rock bridge), Qalaat Faqra (Roman ruins with a 15 m tower) and Chabrouh Dam above Faraya village. Check opening times before you go, and check the weather in winter.
