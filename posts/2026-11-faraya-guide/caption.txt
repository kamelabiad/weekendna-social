Planning a weekend in Faraya? Here is a short guide 🔥⛰️
Found on Weekendna: where to stay, what to see, what to do and where to eat.
1. Acampar Faraya, 45 min from Beirut: wooden bungalows, most with a fireplace, mountain views and a terrace, for 2 to 6 guests. From $80/night.
2. Faraya Village Club, Mzaar: a chimney in every chalet and a heated pool on site. From $150/night.
Places to visit: Chabrouh Dam, the Faqra Roman ruins, Faqra Natural Bridge and the Saint Charbel statue.
Things to do: ski and snowboard at Mzaar when the slopes are open, snowmobile rides with a local operator, and mountain walks and scenic drives.
Where to eat: Jnaynit al Khaweja, Em Sherif Deli, Mario e Mario, Meat the Fish and LTTL BEAN for coffee.
Check opening times before you go, and check the weather in winter.
