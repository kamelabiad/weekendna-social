## Priorities for the next 14 days
1. Lead with genuine, beautiful listing photos or owner-authorized footage. Never use AI-generated property visuals or stock imagery. Do not turn still photos into crude slideshows and call them cinematic.
2. Creative benchmark: Six Senses-style quiet luxury (inspiration only, never copy). Restrained, elegant typography; warm, natural colours; premium composition; purposeful editing; professional licensed audio. If footage is not available, a refined 4:5 photo carousel may be better than a low-quality Reel.
3. November focus: fireplace interiors, warm chalets, mountain hideaways, Faraya/Cedars; avoid summer swimming imagery, unverified snow claims and unsupported prices.
4. Make the first image/frame a real, striking place visual. Avoid logo intros, busy price badges, repetitive animated crops, heavy templates and text-only feed cards.
5. Prioritize save/share CTAs: 'Save this for your next weekend' or 'Send this to your weekend partner'. Do not make comment keywords the primary CTA while comments are zero.
6. Preserve the existing weekly cadence (Mon Discovery Reel; Wed photo-based guide/carousel; Thu Weekend Edit Reel; Fri stay Reel; story-led other days). Do not create extra posts just to meet a daily quota. Verify scheduled posts in posts/log.json to avoid duplicates and owner saturation.
7. Publish English-only. Verify all property facts on Weekendna's current listing page. No unverified availability, snow, pricing, capacity, travel times or amenities.
8. Avoid claiming guaranteed bookings; Weekendna connects enquiries. Never contact owners or run paid promotions without Kamel's permission.

## Quality gate
Reject or request rework for poor-quality zoom slideshows, synthetic background music, busy overlays, weak first frames, unreadable text, unverifiable claims, unlicensed music, reused shots or content that contradicts November seasonality. For Reels, prefer real video footage; if none is available, prefer an excellent carousel over a mediocre Reel.
